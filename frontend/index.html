info ABOUT HORIZONBYTE

HorizonByte is a cutting-edge Neural AI Assistant engineered from the ground up with a custom Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture.

Unlike generic AI platforms, HorizonByte ditches bloated frameworks for raw, high-performance Python processing. It utilizes recursive semantic chunking and FAISS vector indices to ensure context accuracy, driven by the latest Gemini 1.5 & 2.0 models.

Design Philosophy: Rooted in Cyber-Brutalist aesthetics, HorizonByte prioritizes information density, functional rigidity, and surgical precision over organic flourishes. Total technical control in a high-bandwidth terminal.

Rephrase Engine: Embedded within the command interface is a specialized Hinglish-to-English translation module. It interprets natural thoughts typed in Hinglish and restructures them into formal, professional, or casual English tone, allowing writers to express raw concepts and refine them for presentation seamlessly.
